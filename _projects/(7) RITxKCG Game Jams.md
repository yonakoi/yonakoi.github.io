## Overview

I had the opportunity to collaborate with Japanese students on 2 game jams. The first game is called "My Neighbor is Making a Ruckus! How Can I Shut Him Up? I Have No Choice But To Punch Him!", with the theme of punch, done through an online game jam. The second game is called "Fih", with the driving theme of onomatopoeia, done in person at KCG.

## Gameplay

In the first game, the player must hit specific objects to get more points under a certain amount of time. There are also bad objects that the player has to avoid hitting, signified with a red ring. This game is a point and click game, where you punch through the shoji screens to get as many objects as you can.

In the second game, the player has to use their voice to defeat enemies by shouting out different onomatopoeias, such as 'dokan' or 'boom.' It is an endless tower defense game, where the only way to lose is when the player's health reaches zero.

## My Role

For both games, I was mainly in charge of leading the design process as well as debugging. I created all the art in both games, directing the general aesthetics of both games. When I finished my art, I would help clean up code or fix any major bugs. The biggest program I fixed is in the first game, the ability to switch from Japanese UI to English UI and keep consistency throughout the stages.
